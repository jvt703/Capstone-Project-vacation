Vacation Management Application

About the Application

The Vacation Management Application is an Android application designed to help users manage their vacations in a centralized place.

The application allows users to create, edit, search, and view vacation information and excursions. The application also provides vacation and excursion notifications, reporting, and the ability to share vacation information.

Application Features

  • Login authentication
  • Vacation management
  • Excursion management
  • Vacation search
  • Vacation and excursion notifications
  • Vacation reports
  • Vacation information sharing
